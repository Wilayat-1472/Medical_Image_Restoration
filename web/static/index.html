A QUICK GUIDE

From upload to a clearer view.

Choose your image

Upload a single PNG, JPG, TIFF or BMP image, or try the sample X-ray. Use an image at least 32 × 32 pixels and no larger than 4096 × 4096.

Create a restored preview

Click Restore image. The model was trained on chest X-rays with synthetic noise, blur and other image degradation.

Compare and download

Drag the comparison slider, switch between views, and save the restored PNG. Your original file is never changed.

About this preview

The model predicts a correction at 128 × 128 pixels. This correction is applied to your original image, retaining its finer-resolution detail. High-frequency noise may remain. Use lower strength for cleaner inputs and always compare small structures with the original. Downloads are 8-bit PNG previews; original image metadata is not retained. Results on other kinds of medical images have not been validated.
